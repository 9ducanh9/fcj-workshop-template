## Lessons Learned / Contribution

- I gained a deeper understanding of how context engineering affects the performance of AI systems.
- One insight that stayed with me was: "Context quality is more important than context quantity." This idea influenced how I think about building AI systems and later shaped several architectural decisions in my AWS Bootcamp final project.
- I learned how CloudFront improves performance, scalability, and user experience through edge locations.
- The multi-agent credit scoring case study provided valuable insights into enterprise AI architecture and governance.
- The UTMorpho presentation demonstrated how AI can accelerate product prototyping and UI development.
- The Amazon Quick Suite session introduced practical approaches for business process automation.
- The LLM non-determinism session improved my understanding of the limitations and behavior of production AI systems.
