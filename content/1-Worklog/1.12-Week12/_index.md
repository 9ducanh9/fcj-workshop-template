## Weekly Summary

In the final week, the team focused on completing documentation, testing, the demo flow, and all end-of-term deliverables for LiveCap. Key work included updating requirements documentation, system design, use cases, data models, KPIs, the UAT checklist, AWS architecture, security/monitoring/cost optimization sections, and each member's individual contributions. I learned that completing a project means more than finishing the technical implementation — it also requires presenting it clearly, documenting it thoroughly, and evaluating it honestly. Through the Information Systems role, I gained a deeper appreciation for the importance of connecting business requirements, system design, data modeling, KPIs, UAT, and real-world implementation into a coherent whole. After 12 weeks — six weeks of AWS self-study and six weeks of implementing LiveCap — I came away with practical experience in building a cloud-based application using real-time processing, AWS managed services, storage, monitoring, security, and organized documentation practices.
