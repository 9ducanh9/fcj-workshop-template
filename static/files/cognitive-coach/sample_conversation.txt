Mentor: Why did you choose this project?
Student: I want to build an AI assistant for communication.
Mentor: Why is that useful?
Student: Because many students and junior engineers cannot explain ideas clearly under pressure.
Mentor: Why should this use AWS?
Student: AWS provides storage, transcription, AI analysis, workflow orchestration, and monitoring as managed services.
Mentor: What happens if AI is wrong?
Student: The system should be used as coaching feedback, not final truth. The user must review the output.
Mentor: Why is the project not real time?
Student: Real-time audio adds latency, privacy, and demo risk. Post-conversation reflection is safer and more realistic for a bootcamp final project.
